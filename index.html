HOW TO PLAY

  1. Walk with the joystick (or tap a spot). Your pets follow you!
  2. Care: tap your pet or ❤️ CARE for a close-up. Drag on your pet to pet her. FEED, PLAY fetch, WASH, BRUSH, SLEEP, CALL and DRESS.
  3. Tricks: in care mode, swipe the screen: down = Sit, up = Jump, circle = Spin, double-tap = Wave, sideways = Roll Over. 3 good lessons and it’s learned!
  4. Needs go down slowly. Pets get sad if you forget them, but never sick. Happy pets level up and grow from Baby to Adult.
  5. Coins: earn them from care, daily rewards, walks (pets dig up surprises!) and the 4 mini games at the Pet Park.
  6. Town: Pet Shop (food, toys, accessories, furniture, eggs, adoption corner), Vet & Groomer, Adoption Center, Pet Park and the Beach.
  7. Home: tap DECOR to place furniture. Pets you leave home play there.
  8. Animal Chaos: be the pet! Knock things over, steal snacks and socks, leave muddy paw prints, confuse your owner and escape Animal Control. Play from the title screen, the ☰ menu or the Pet Park (co-op with friends too).
  9. Friends: host a town or join with a code. Visit, PLAYDATE, send gifts and play mini games together or against each other.
