# Grok Pets 🐾

A cozy 3D pet-raising sim for phones (and desktop): adopt, care for and train cute pets, decorate your home,
shop at the Pet Shop, play mini games at the Pet Park and hang out online with up to 2 friends.

**Play:** https://lizethbran13-cmyk.github.io/grok-pets/

- 18 species (dogs (incl. bulldog), cats, rats, tortoise, bunny, hamster, parrot, goldfish, hedgehog + robot dog, mini dragon, unicorn pony, galaxy cat from eggs)
- Family pets: Candy, Martina, Luna, Pi-rat and Snowie
- Up-close care: pet, feed, fetch, wash, brush, sleep, call by name, dress up, teach tricks with gestures
- Mini games: Frisbee Catch, Pet Race, Pet Show, Treasure Dig
- Online co-op (PeerJS, 5-letter room codes, 3 players max)

Part of the Grok Arcade. Built with three.js r128.
